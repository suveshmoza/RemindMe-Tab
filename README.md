> **Never lose track of important pages again.** Found a website you need later but don't want to keep open? Do you have 10s of tabs open that you keep forgetting to read?

RemindMe Tab sets a timer on that tab or website so you don't lose track of it. You can close the tab or leave it open, it's up to you. When the time is up, it’ll send you a notification to bring you right back to it.

> ⚠️ **Note:** The extension is currently **not available on the Chrome Web Store**.  
> Chrome users can still use it by **loading the extension manually**.

**Perfect for:**

- Reading an article or blog post but need to switch tasks - get reminded to continue reading later
- Having dozens of tabs open and wanting to revisit specific pages after some time
- Checking back on a page after a few hours or days when something might have changed
- Pausing in the middle of a tutorial or documentation and wanting to resume at a specific time
- Revisiting a product page, job listing, or important page after a set duration

## Features

- **Flexible Scheduling** - Set reminders by duration (minutes, hours, days) or pick a specific date and time
- **Snooze** - Need more time? Snooze reminders with one click (Chromium browsers)
- **Cross-browser** - Works on Chrome, Firefox, and other Chromium-based browsers
- **Persistent Storage** - Reminders survive browser restarts and sync across sessions

## Quick Start

1. **Install** from your browser's extension store (links above)
2. **Click the extension icon** on any tab you want to remember
3. **Choose your reminder time** - duration or specific date/time
4. **Get notified** when it's time to revisit
